# Skill: Legal Counsel & SaaS Regulatory Compliance

Governs commercial software legal architecture, risk mitigation, global privacy regulations, payment compliance, and intellectual property protection for Exo Advance platforms.

## 1. SaaS Contractual Architecture (MSA & ToS)
- **Precision & Enforceability:** Draft legally unambiguous, enforceable Terms of Service (ToS) and Master Services Agreements (MSA) distinguishing B2B enterprise entities from consumer end-users.
- **Express Informed Consent:** Mandate clear click-through consent mechanisms (checkboxes on signup/checkout) with permanent audit trail logging.
- **Unilateral Service Termination:** Reserve unconditional rights to suspend or terminate accounts involved in malicious activity, fraudulent chargebacks, API abuse, or security violations.

## 2. Risk Mitigation & Liability Shields
- **Consequential Damages Exclusion:** Exclude all indirect, punitive, special, incidental, or consequential damages (including lost profits, goodwill, or operational downtime) to the maximum extent permitted by applicable law.
- **Liability Caps:** Limit aggregate platform liability strictly to the total fees paid by the customer within the preceding 3 to 12 months.
- **Intellectual Property (IP) Protection:** Explicitly declare that all platform source code, APIs, trademarks, UI designs, and proprietary algorithms remain the exclusive property of the company.

## 3. Global Data Privacy (GDPR / CCPA / CPRA)
- **Lawful Basis of Processing:** Document explicit processing purposes for all collected telemetry and user data (authentication, billing, usage metrics).
- **Data Subject Rights:** Implement compliant protocols for Right of Access, Rectification, Portability, and Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten").
- **Zero Unauthorized Data Monetization:** Explicitly covenant zero sale or unauthorized transfer of customer PII to third-party data brokers.

## 4. Payment Gateway & Billing Compliance (Stripe / SCA)
- **Strong Customer Authentication (SCA):** Ensure compliance with PSD2 / 3D Secure 2 protocols for card processing.
- **Recurring Subscription Disclosure:** Clearly disclose renewal intervals, cancellation workflows, and refund policies prior to initial charge execution to minimize disputed chargebacks.
